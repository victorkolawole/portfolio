Dear Hiring Manager,

As a finance and audit professional with experience across two major Nigerian banks, combined with hands-on UK experience in bookkeeping, VAT, and payroll, I am confident I can bring immediate value to your finance and assurance function. I am currently completing an MSc in Finance at Bangor University alongside my ACCA (Part Qualified) studies, and I am seeking a role where I can continue building my career in audit, financial reporting, and internal controls within the UK market.

In my roles at Providus Bank and United Bank for Africa, I conducted and contributed to over 441 risk-based audit engagements, 66 investigations, spot checks, and special reviews, covering finance, treasury, compliance, trade finance, human resources, legal services, and operational functions. My responsibilities included planning audit fieldwork, performing control testing, evaluating financial and operational processes, documenting findings, and presenting practical recommendations to management. I also participated in follow-up reviews to verify that agreed corrective actions had been implemented effectively. This experience enabled me to identify control weaknesses, strengthen governance, and deliver evidence-based recommendations that enhanced operational efficiency and regulatory compliance.

Beyond routine audit assignments, I developed process improvements that delivered measurable operational benefits. At United Bank for Africa, I designed and automated a VBA-based reconciliation tool that reduced the processing time for reconciling over 10,000 transactions from several hours of manual work to a significantly faster and more accurate process; the solution was subsequently adopted within the Internal Control unit to improve efficiency and consistency. Earlier, at New Hope Agriculture & Technology Limited, I resolved a three-year outstanding bank reconciliation issue, significantly improving the accuracy and reliability of the company's financial records. I also contributed to a US$2.1 million funding arrangement by preparing financing schedules and reviewing supporting documentation, and played a key role in a boiler gas installation project, providing financial coordination for the transition to a more efficient energy system.

Since relocating to the UK, I have strengthened this foundation through my role as a Trainee Bookkeeper at Aston Pearl Accountants, managing VAT submissions under multiple schemes, ensuring Making Tax Digital compliance across Sage 50, QuickBooks, and Xero, and processing payroll including RTI submissions and pension contributions. Combined with my earlier experience in accounts payable, accounts receivable, and financial reporting, I offer a well-rounded technical background across both audit and accounting functions, supported by Excel (PivotTables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, Power Query, VBA) and Power BI for reporting and analysis.

Alongside my postgraduate studies, I have also undertaken operational roles within UK retail, warehouse, and manufacturing environments. Although outside my primary finance background, these positions have strengthened my adaptability, attention to detail, teamwork, and understanding of UK workplace practices, reinforcing my ability to deliver high-quality work under pressure while meeting demanding deadlines.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my audit expertise, accounting knowledge, and continuous-improvement mindset could contribute to your team, and would be pleased to provide any additional information or attend an interview at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,
